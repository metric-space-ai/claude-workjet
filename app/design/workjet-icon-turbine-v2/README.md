# Workjet turbine mark v2

Four intentionally turbine-first directions. None uses a paper plane, arrow,
funnel, plug, play triangle, letterform, or generic AI sparkle.

1. **Turbofan Front** — the strongest immediate turbine read. A front-facing
   fan, casing ring, hub and eight swept blades survive reduction well. The
   three small lateral streaks add intake-to-thrust direction at large sizes;
   at 16 px they collapse into one useful exhaust notch rather than detail.
2. **Axial Afterburner** — a literal side cutaway: intake bell, four compressor
   stages, core axis, nozzle and hot plume. It communicates Workjet's full
   intake/compress/accelerate story best at 32–256 px. At 16 px it reads as a
   jet engine silhouette, but individual stages reduce to three dark rhythm
   marks; it is weaker as a tiny menu-bar mark than direction 1.
3. **Dual Spool** — a more abstract side elevation with cold-to-hot axial flow,
   compressor/turbine stators and a front spool. It is distinctive at app-icon
   size, but the centerline dominates at 16 px and risks reading as a capsule.
4. **Compressor Crown** — a heavier, more geometric rotor with an attached hot
   exhaust chamber. It remains bold at 16 px, though the thrust attachment can
   be mistaken for a tab. The rotor itself remains unambiguously mechanical.

## Recommendation

Use **Turbofan Front** as the primary Workjet brand and menu-bar template. Its
fan ring and swept rotor are the only concept here that reads as a turbine at a
glance without relying on an engine side profile. For the macOS template image,
flatten the casing, blades and hub to one color and retain the exhaust notch as
negative/positive silhouette. Use the supplied color treatment for the app
icon.

If the brand must communicate the process more literally than the object, use
**Axial Afterburner** in large product surfaces while keeping Turbofan Front in
the menu bar.
